PastVault
SCHOOL-WIDE PAST QUESTION PLATFORM · STUDENT SHOWCASE

Verified past questions, in one place.

Past questions and notes circulate through scattered WhatsApp chats and random uploads — with no way to tell what's genuine. Students end up revising from wrong or fake material with no way to check it. PastVault fixes that for every department in the school, not just one.

THE PROBLEM

No trusted, central source for past questions — material spreads informally with zero quality control, department by department.

THE SOLUTION

Every department gets its own vault of past questions, organized by level and semester — but nothing goes live until an admin reviews and approves it.
